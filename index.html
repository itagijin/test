Why we exist

We build bridges from the classics to the community.

Thousands of hours of classical speech sit locked in archives, courts, and classrooms across Africa. Scholar-trained speech recognition for classical tongues, paired with natural African-language output, solves this. We prioritize the communities that never had a translation option.

Access

Interpretation bridges for courts, clinics, museums, and civic services where translation was never an option.

How it works

From archive reel to living voice.

Step 01

Upload

Drop in audio, a sermon, a lecture, an archive reel, or capture live speech in the field.

Step 02

Transcribe

ASR tuned to classical phonology and metre, checked against scholarly corpora.

Step 03

Translate

Context-aware machine translation, reviewed by native-speaker linguists and classicists.

Step 04

Deliver

Text, subtitles, or natural audio in the African language of your community's choice.

Privacy Policy

How babalang collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Who we are

babalang (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides transcription of speech in classical languages and translation into African languages. For any privacy question, contact privacy@babalang.org.

2. Information we collect

  • Audio you upload. Recordings you submit, the language pairs you select, and the transcripts we generate.
  • Contact details. Name, email, organisation, and message content.
  • Usage data. Pages visited, features used, device type, and error logs.
  • Locally stored preferences. Demo presentations you add on this site are stored only in your browser.

3. How we use your information

  • To process your audio into transcripts and deliver outputs.
  • To operate and improve our models.
  • To respond to enquiries and manage partnerships.
  • To send the newsletter you subscribed to (opt-out anytime).

4. Your audio and consent

By uploading audio, you confirm you have the right to submit it. We do not use your audio to train models unless you opt in. You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. Sharing

We never sell personal data. We share information only with service providers, partner institutions (at your request), and authorities (where required by law).

6. Retention & Deletion

Uploaded audio is retained for 30 days, then permanently deleted unless longer retention is agreed. Transcripts are kept in your workspace.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and audit logging.

8. Your rights

You may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete your personal data. Submit requests to privacy@babalang.org.

9. Cookies & Local Storage

We use strictly necessary storage to keep the site working. No third-party advertising trackers.

10. Children's Privacy

For learners under 18, we collect data only with verifiable school and guardian consent.

11. International Transfers

Data may be processed in our operating countries with appropriate safeguards.

12. Changes

We may update this policy with a new “last updated” date.

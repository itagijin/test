Why we exist

Bridges from the classics to the community.

Thousands of hours of classical speech — sermons, oral histories, legal maxims, epic verse — sit locked in archives, courts and classrooms across Africa, because nobody has bridged them into living languages. babalang builds that bridge: scholar-trained speech recognition for classical tongues, paired with natural African-language output. We go first to the communities that never had a translation option — and we hand the tools to schools.

Privacy Policy

How babalang collects, uses and protects your information when you use our speech-to-text, translation and education services.

1. Who we are

babalang (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides transcription of speech in classical languages and translation into African languages, together with access services and education initiatives. Our offices are in Nairobi, Accra and Cape Town. For any privacy question, contact privacy@babalang.org.

2. Information we collect

  • Audio you upload. Recordings you submit through our transcription bench or deliver to us as a partner, together with the language pairs you select and the transcripts and translations we generate from them.
  • Contact details. Name, email address, organisation and message content when you write to us, request a classroom kit or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Account information (where you create one): name, email, password hash and institutional affiliation.
  • Usage data. Pages visited, features used, device and browser type, approximate location derived from IP address, and error logs.
  • Locally stored preferences. Demo presentations you add on this site are stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and are never transmitted to us.

3. How we use your information

  • To process your audio into transcripts and translations and deliver the outputs you request.
  • To operate, maintain and improve our speech-recognition and translation models.
  • To respond to enquiries, provide support and manage partnerships and education programmes.
  • To send the newsletter you subscribed to (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To meet legal obligations and protect the security and integrity of the service.

4. Your audio and consent

By uploading audio, you confirm that you have the right to submit it — including, where required, the consent of the speakers recorded. We do not use your uploaded audio to train models unless you explicitly opt in to the Open Corpus Africa contribution programme, in which case recordings are pseudonymised and released under a clearly stated open licence. You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. Sharing

We never sell personal data. We share information only with:

  • Service providers who process data on our instructions under contract (hosting, storage, analytics);
  • Partner institutions, where you have asked us to deliver outputs to them;
  • Authorities, where required by law.

6. Retention & deletion

Uploaded audio is retained for 30 days to support quality review and redelivery, then permanently deleted, unless you ask for earlier deletion or agree to longer retention as part of a project. Transcripts and translations are retained in your workspace for as long as your account or project is active. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls, audit logging and least-privilege access for staff. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable and appropriate measures for the sensitivity of linguistic and heritage data.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the Kenyan Data Protection Act, the South African POPIA and the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Submit requests to privacy@babalang.org; we respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies & local storage

We use strictly necessary storage to keep the site working (for example, remembering demos you have added in your browser). We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

10. Children's privacy

Our education programmes may involve learners under 18. Where they do, we collect personal data only with verifiable school and guardian consent, minimise what we collect, and never use learner data for profiling or marketing.

11. International transfers

Data may be processed in the countries where we operate and in the locations of our contracted processors. Where data crosses borders, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced on this page with a new “last updated” date before they take effect.
